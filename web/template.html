How the numbers are built

  • Incident type, context, identity and community can each hold several labels; every label is counted once per incident, so breakdown bars can add up to more than the total.
  • Provincial rates divide incidents by Statistics Canada 2021 Census population.
  • Missing province and city values are kept as "Not specified" rather than dropped.

Read with care

  • Media-reported. An incident is here only if the news covered it, so spikes also reflect news attention.
  • English-language sources. None of the articles come from French-language outlets, so Quebec is very likely under-counted.
  • Sparse fields. Victim gender is missing for 65% of incidents and province for 6%.
